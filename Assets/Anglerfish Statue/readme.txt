Some technical notes:

Textures are in Allegorithmic's fantastic and versatile Substance format. Unity 2017.3 still has native support, but starting with 2018.1 that comes to an end and Allegorithmic will instead supply a free plug-in on the Unity Asset Store.

In case you are currently using the Unity 2018 Beta, you will have to get the free Substance Player to customize and export the textures for now.

Available at: https://www.allegorithmic.com/products/substance-player

You will also have to use the player to load presets regardless of the Unity Version you use, but you can save them from within Unity.

anglerfish_maps Substance:

All the various controls as well as the outputs should be self-explanatory. I've tried my best to describe their functions right in their names. Color High/Low always correspond to opposite ends of a gradient on the part it is named after. 

Some general info:

Albedo/Base Color is derived from the Gradient Map, Material Masks and Curvature.
If you adjust the Gradient Levels and Curvature controls, the Albedo will change accordingly.

The Gradient Map is based on rotated Position Maps in BSphere format and linear gradients, aligned to cover certain parts of the model in useful orientations. They offer not just a nice base for coloring, but adjusting their levels allows you to further isolate specific areas on the mesh, which makes for great masks well. Especially when further combined with the painted Material Masks.

The Material Masks map is an authored map in linear grayscale, where different areas of the model have been assigned values in 0.05 increments. 


anglerfish_maskCreator

Lets you isolate single masks, or a range of them. One output is the mask in black and white, the other blends the mask over a chosen background texture (or none) in the color you picked.



anglerfish_masks Substance:

A simplified version of the Material Masks map which lets you assign colors to each area.
